The presence status is a key feature to make the real-time platform engaging and interactive for the users.

The real-time presence platform leverages the [heartbeat signal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heartbeat_%28computing%29) signal to check the status of the client in real time. The presence status is broadcast to the clients using the persistent [server-sent events](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/Server-sent_events/Using_server-sent_events) (**SSE**) connections on the [real-time platform](https://systemdesign.one/live-comment-system-design/).
