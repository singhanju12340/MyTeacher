## 1. Functional Requirements
**Issue Management:**
	Create, update, delete, and view issues (bugs, tasks, user stories, etc.
	Track issue status, priority, assignee, and labels.
	Commenting on issues, attachments, and history/audit logs.

**Project & Workflow Management:**
	Create projects, manage boards (Kanban, Scrum).
	Define and customize workflows, including statuses and transitions.
	Support for sprints, backlog management, and releases.
